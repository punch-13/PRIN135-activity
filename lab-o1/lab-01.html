Chicken Adobo

Chicken adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the chicken in a bowl and add the soy sauce.
  2. Heat a pan and cook the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Pour in the soy sauce, vinegar, and water.
  5. Add the bay leaves and black pepper.
  6. Cover and simmer until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  7. Serve the chicken adobo with steamed rice.
